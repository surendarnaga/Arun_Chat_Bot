# Split Leather Validation Findings
Author: Hannah Cole
Date: 2026-04-22
Plant: Birmingham
Type: Validation Summary

Validation testing on split leather seat coverings identified three recurring findings.

Findings:
- Edge wear appeared earlier than expected after accelerated abrasion testing.
- Surface finish inconsistency was reported across two incoming lots.
- Stitch alignment deviation exceeded the cosmetic threshold on 6 of 40 samples.

Overall conclusion:
The material remains acceptable for controlled pilot release, but full release requires tighter incoming inspection and supplier corrective action on surface finish consistency.
