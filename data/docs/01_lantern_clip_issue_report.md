# Lantern Clip Incident Report
Author: Priya Raman
Date: 2026-05-14
Plant: Coventry
Type: Incident Report

The lantern clip assembly issue was observed during final trim validation on the rear interior module. The clip failed retention checks after thermal cycling. Initial review suggests dimensional drift in the supplier batch from week 18.

Key findings:
- 14 units showed partial disengagement after vibration testing.
- The issue is more visible on the right-hand side trim panel.
- Rework was required before shipment hold could be lifted.
- Containment action was opened with the supplier quality team.

Recommendation:
Run an updated gauge validation and compare clip tolerance measurements against the approved baseline from March 2026.
