# Seat Trim Know-How Guide
Author: Martin Hughes
Date: 2026-06-05
Version: 3.2
Type: Know-How Document

This document describes how to identify early warning indicators for seat trim fit issues during pre-line inspection.

Highlights:
- Check clip seating force before final fixation.
- Confirm leather surface alignment under daylight lamp inspection.
- Record supplier batch references in the validation sheet.

This is the latest released know-how document from Martin Hughes for trim validation activities.
