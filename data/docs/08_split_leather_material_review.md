# Split Leather Material Review
Author: Hannah Cole
Date: 2026-04-25
Type: Material Review

The split leather material review confirmed that the main validation concerns were cosmetic consistency and early wear under stress conditions.

Summary:
- Surface finish variation was visible between adjacent seat panels.
- Abrasion resistance was below the expected durability target in accelerated testing.
- The review recommended tighter controls on incoming lot approval before full production release.

This note supports the broader split leather validation findings and should be used together with the formal validation summary.
